About Daudh Run Club

Founded to make running social, safe, and consistent. We welcome beginners, casual joggers, and competitive athletes. Our core values: inclusivity, safety, and consistency.

What we offer

  • Weekly group runs (3x) with pacers.
  • Structured training plans for 5K, 10K, half marathon.
  • Coach-led interval and technique sessions.
  • Community events, social runs, and workshops.

Training programs

Beginner 3K (8 weeks)

Build from walk/run to continuous 3K. Group meets twice weekly, plus one long run.

Start this program →
Coach's tip

Consistency beats intensity early on — aim for 3 quality sessions a week and gradually increase volume by no more than 10% per week.

Safety

All runs have a sweeper, basic first-aid kit at meeting points, and mandatory visible clothing for night runs.
